My specialty

The sitter for medical & special-needs cats

A missed dose or an unnoticed change can matter a lot for a cat in recovery or on a schedule. This is the work I built my reputation on.

Post-surgery care

Bandage and cone checks, wound watching, restricted-activity routines, and medication kept exactly on schedule while your cat heals — with honest updates if anything looks off.

Twice-daily medication

Morning and evening visits timed to your cat's dosing schedule — pills, liquids, drops, and ointments given calmly and confirmed to you after every single visit.

Senior cats

Gentle, unhurried visits for older cats — appetite, litter box, mobility, and comfort checked every time, because small changes in a senior cat are worth catching early.

Why I do this

Meet Fettuccine — my cat, and my training

My own cat, Fettuccine, is an orange tabby I adopted from a local shelter. She's FIV-positive, which means her health gets monitored closely: appetite, energy, weight, any small change that could signal something bigger.

That daily habit — notice early, act calmly, keep the routine exact — is what I bring to your cat. Medication schedules, post-surgery checks, and special-needs routines aren't add-ons for me. They're how I already live with my own cat.

She's also the face of this page, pink collar, heart tag and all.

The Full Visit

Everything in the Essential, plus unhurried company for your cat.
